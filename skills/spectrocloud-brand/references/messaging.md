## Competitive Positioning

### Key Differentiators vs Competition

- **vs Red Hat OpenShift**: Non-opinionated, any distro, no vendor lock-in
- **vs VMware Tanzu**: Purpose-built for multi-environment K8s at scale
- **vs Rancher/SUSE**: Full-stack declarative management from OS to app (patented)
- **vs Cloud-native (EKS/AKS/GKE)**: Unified multi-cloud + on-prem + edge management

### Messaging Don'ts

- Do NOT position as a startup -- we are a technology scale-up
- Do NOT use space/astronaut metaphors in formal materials (OK for internal culture/swag)
- Do NOT use blue/purple color schemes (old brand)
- Do NOT say "opinionated" -- Palette is explicitly "non-opinionated"
